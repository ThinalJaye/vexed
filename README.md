## Project Overview

VEXED is a full-lifecycle business software project to design, architect, build, test, and launch an international-quality streetwear and custom DTF T-shirt printing e-commerce platform tailored for Sri Lanka.

This repository is managed as a professional, phase-gated software engineering engagement. Each phase completes only after formal review and explicit Business Owner approval.

## Information Classification Standard (`CONFIRMED`)

All documentation in this repository tags statements with one of three statuses:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **`CONFIRMED`** | Explicitly provided or approved by the Business Owner. |
| **`PROPOSED`** | Recommended by the project team; subject to Business Owner approval. |
| **`UNRESOLVED`** | Open item requiring clarification, business data, or formal sign-off. |

---

## Project Governance Summary

- **Sequential Execution:** One approved phase at a time; no phase begins without Business Owner sign-off.
- **No Premature Implementation:** Application code, frameworks, dependencies, databases, and designs are deferred to their designated roadmap phases (STEP 03–STEP 11).
- **Source of Truth:** All decisions, scope changes, and open questions are tracked in `project-management/`.
