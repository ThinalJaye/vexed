# 00 – Project Vision

> **Document Status:** STEP 01 Deliverable (Permanent Vision Baseline)
> **Owner:** Project Manager (Accountable) | Business Owner (Approver)

---

## 1. Brand Identity (`CONFIRMED`)

- **Brand Name:** VEXED
- **Tagline:** *NOT MADE TO BLEND IN.*
- **Core Business:** Premium Streetwear and Custom DTF (Direct-to-Film) T-Shirt Printing.
- **Initial Target Market:** Sri Lanka.
- **Primary Currency:** Sri Lankan Rupee (`LKR`).
- **Overarching Goal:** Deliver an international-quality, modern streetwear e-commerce brand experience—distinctly elevated above generic e-commerce templates.

---

## 2. Product & Service Vision (`CONFIRMED`)

VEXED serves both style-conscious streetwear buyers and customers seeking bespoke apparel printing through a unified digital storefront offering:

- Premium oversized T-shirts
- DTF printed T-shirts
- Ready-made graphic T-shirt designs
- Custom-designed T-shirts
- Customer-specific printing services
- Customer-uploaded artwork orders
- End-to-end online T-shirt ordering

## 4. Product & Engineering Guiding Principles (`PROPOSED`)

1. **Brand-First Commerce:** Editorial storytelling and high-craft motion must enhance—never obstruct—product discovery, custom requests, and checkout conversion.
2. **Mobile Excellence & Performance Discipline:** Animations, parallax, and fashion photography must remain smooth and fast on Sri Lankan mobile networks and mid-range smartphones.
3. **Operational Integrity:** Manual bank transfer verification is a deliberate business safeguard. The software must make verification clear, auditable, and mistake-proof for the owner while keeping order status transparent for customers.
4. **Phased, Maintainable Delivery:** Build a clean, extensible architecture where MVP launches cleanly and future enhancements (e.g., automated payment gateways, advanced design studios, loyalty) plug in without rework.
5. **Quality Built In:** QA, security, privacy, and accessibility are integrated across every phase rather than bolted on before launch.
