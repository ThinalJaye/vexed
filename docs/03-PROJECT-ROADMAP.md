## 2. Phase Governance Rules (`CONFIRMED`)

1. **Strict Gatekeeping:** A phase transitions to `COMPLETED – AWAITING BUSINESS OWNER APPROVAL` only after all phase deliverables are written, verified, and reviewed.
2. **Sequential Progression:** No technical implementation (Next.js setup, packages, code, schemas, APIs) begins before its designated phase is approved.
3. **Continuous QA Integration:** QA reviews requirements (STEP 02), designs (STEP 04), architecture (STEP 05), test plans (STEP 06), every build increment (STEPs 07–11), full UAT (STEP 12), and production smoke checks (STEP 13).
4. **Controlled Change:** Insights discovered in later phases that affect earlier baselines must be formally logged in `project-management/DECISION-LOG.md` and approved by the Business Owner.
