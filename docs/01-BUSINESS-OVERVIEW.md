VEXED operates across two tightly connected product streams:

1. **Ready-Made Streetwear Apparel (`CONFIRMED`):**

## 4. End-to-End Customer Ordering Journey (`CONFIRMED`)

The confirmed 17-step customer and fulfillment workflow is:

1. Customer visits the VEXED website.
2. Customer explores T-shirt collections and designs.
3. Customer selects a product.
4. Customer selects size, colour, and quantity.
5. Customer can request a custom design (and/or upload customer artwork).
6. Customer adds products to the shopping cart.
7. Customer enters customer contact and delivery details.
8. Customer places the order.
9. System generates a **unique order number**.
10. Customer sees the order summary and VEXED **business bank details**.
11. Customer makes a bank transfer externally via their bank.
12. Customer opens **WhatsApp** via an **order-specific prepared message** generated by the platform.
13. Customer attaches their bank deposit slip / transfer receipt in WhatsApp.
14. **Business Owner manually verifies** receipt of funds in the business bank account.
15. **Administrator updates** the payment status and order status in the system.
16. Order progresses through **printing, packaging, and delivery**.
17. Customer can **check order status** online.

### Critical Payment Verification Rule (`CONFIRMED`)

> **WhatsApp communication is NOT automatic payment verification.**
> Receiving a WhatsApp message or slip attachment does not mark an order as paid. The Business Owner must manually confirm actual receipt of funds in the bank account before an administrator updates the payment status to verified/paid and authorizes production.

## 5. Proposed Order & Payment Lifecycle (`PROPOSED`)

To support Steps 8–17 cleanly, the following separated status tracks are proposed for validation in STEP 02:

- **Payment Status (`PROPOSED`):**
  `Awaiting Payment` → `Slip Submitted (Pending Verification)` → `Payment Verified` *(or `Payment Rejected` / `Refunded`)*
- **Fulfillment Status (`PROPOSED`):**
  `Order Placed` → `Artwork / Design Review` *(custom orders only)* → `Confirmed for Production` → `Printing` → `Packaging` → `Dispatched / Out for Delivery` → `Delivered` *(or `Cancelled`)*

Exact state transition rules, cancellation windows, unpaid order expiry times, and custom-design approval steps are `UNRESOLVED` and will be finalized in STEP 02 (`OQ-011` – `OQ-014`).

## 6. Operational & Business Constraints

- **Manual Verification Bottleneck (`PROPOSED` observation):** Because payment verification is manual by design, the Admin Dashboard must make matching orders by unique order number, amount (`LKR`), and customer name fast and error-resistant.
- **Custom Artwork Quality (`PROPOSED` observation):** Customer-uploaded artwork may have low resolution, unsupported formats, or copyright/content issues; file validation rules and an owner review step are required before DTF printing (`UNRESOLVED`: `OQ-008`, `OQ-013`).
- **Delivery & Courier Integration (`UNRESOLVED`):** Whether VEXED uses a local courier partner, in-house delivery, flat-rate vs. district-based `LKR` shipping fees, or Cash-on-Delivery (COD) as an alternative is not yet defined (`OQ-010`, `OQ-015`).
- **Return, Refund & Custom Print Policies (`UNRESOLVED`):** Legal and consumer policies (especially non-returnability of custom-printed items unless defective) must be confirmed by the owner in STEP 02 (`OQ-016`).
